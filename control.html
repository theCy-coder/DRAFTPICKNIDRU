How this works

Each button shows its key: the top letter row is the blue team, the middle row the red team, the bottom row the map. Keys only work on this tab, never while typing in a box, and can be switched off with Hotkeys at the top. One press plays the animation once on the scoreboard overlay. Nothing plays by itself. Turtle, Lord and turret presses also add one to that team’s counter above.

What does this do?

One press adds the win, bumps the game number, clears the draft and takes you to the Draft tab. If that win decides the series, it only adds the win. It also clears the picks, which is why the MVP comes first. Pressed the wrong one? Use Undo on the Draft tab, or the − next to the score.

What is this?

A full-screen scene for the wait between the draft and the game. The ten picked heroes stay on screen, blue along the top and red along the bottom, and each card takes turns showing the hero, the player and the hero’s season win rate. Between the two rows it rotates through the matchup, how the two drafts compare, the bans with their ban rate, both teams’ results and head to head, and the season’s most contested heroes. A slide with nothing to show yet is skipped. It follows the show design.

Season file

Export saves everything on this tab to a file. Import replaces it with a file you exported earlier.

Where does the season show?

In the middle of the draft overlay, when the centre stage (Settings → Look) is set to “Season info”. Any section left empty is skipped. Seconds per page is how long each page stays up.

Teams

How teams are used

Create each team once. Its players go in the card below, and on the Setup tab you can drop a whole team (name, tag, logo and players) onto a side in one go.

Players

How player pictures work

Grouped by team; change a player’s team with the dropdown on their row. A photo shows wherever the player’s name does (lineups, pick spotlight, MVP and waiting screens), matched by the nickname typed on the Draft tab. The picture dropdown on each row chooses what is shown for that player: the default picture, their own uploaded photo, their team’s logo, or nothing. The same dropdown uploads or deletes the player’s photo. Uploaded photos are cropped to an upright 4:5 portrait.

Hero records

Where these numbers come from

Counted for you: each time you press who won on the Live game tab, that game’s picks and bans are added. The draft overlay shows a hero’s record when it is picked or banned. Undo takes a game back out, and the records travel with Export and Import.

Standings

How the standings are worked out

Every scheduled match with a score counts as a win for one team and a loss for the other. Playoff matches are left out: a pairing that is in the Bracket is treated as a playoff, taking its latest scheduled match. Teams level on points are ordered by wins, then by games won minus games lost. Untick the box to type the table yourself.

Schedule

About dates and scores

Dates and times are Philippine time and show that way for everyone, whatever the computer’s own clock is set to. Matches are listed in date order. “Score” can be a result such as 2 – 1, or left empty for a match still to play.

Bracket

How the bracket is laid out

Matches with the same round name share a column, in the order the rounds first appear. Up to four rounds of four matches fit on a page.
