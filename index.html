MLBB Overlay

Control panel Draft Scoreboard MVP Waiting Loading

Start

  1. Double-click MLBB Overlay Server.exe (or Start Overlay Server.bat, which does the same) and leave its window open while you stream. The control panel opens in your browser.
  2. In OBS add a Browser source for each overlay, all 1920 × 1080:
    SourceURLUse it in
    Waitinghttp://localhost:8777/idle.htmlYour starting-soon / break scene (full screen)
    Drafthttp://localhost:8777/draft.htmlYour draft / pick-ban scene
    Loadinghttp://localhost:8777/loading.htmlBetween the draft and the game (full screen)
    Scoreboardhttp://localhost:8777/scoreboard.htmlYour in-game scene
    MVPhttp://localhost:8777/mvp.htmlYour post-game scene (full screen)
  3. Run everything from the control panel. The overlays update on their own. The same links, with Copy buttons, are on Settings → OBS links, and the server window lists them when it starts.

Tip: in OBS, Docks → Custom Browser Docks lets you add http://localhost:8777/control.html as a dock so the control panel sits inside OBS.

Running a show

  • Before the show: put the season in once on the Season tab (or Import a season file), and choose the show design in Settings → Look. On the Setup tab, pick today’s game from Scheduled match and both sides fill in with their logo, tag and players; or choose each side from its Team dropdown. A team that is not in the season can be typed in by hand, and Clear empties a side.
  • Click a hero (or type part of the name and press Enter) to lock it into the highlighted slot. The draft moves to the next phase by itself and follows the tournament order.
  • Click any slot to fix a mistake, right-click to clear it, and drag one pick onto another to swap them during the adjustment phase.
  • Undo steps back one action. Next game clears the draft and bumps the game number; on the Live game tab, press the team that won and it adds the win and starts the next game in one go.
  • Draft mode: Normal or Fearless. In Fearless, heroes picked in earlier games of the series are locked. “Next game” records the finished game’s picks; “Reset series” clears them.
  • The control panel has five tabs. The show runs left to right through Setup (teams and match), Draft (picks and bans only) and Live game (score, announcements, MVP, who won); Settings (look, draft rules, scoreboard, OBS links) and Season (teams, players, standings, schedule, bracket) are set up beforehand and are split into sub-tabs. Keys 1–5 switch between them.
  • The strip under the tabs is always in view: the series score, whose turn it is in the draft, and the On screen switches that show or hide the draft, scoreboard and MVP overlays from any tab. The small ? links open the explanation for that card.
  • Announcements: on the Live game tab, buttons such as “Turtle spawned” or “Lord slain” play a short animated banner on the scoreboard overlay. To use your own artwork, see the note at the top of js/events.js.
  • Objectives: the Live game tab counts turrets, turtles and lords for each team and shows them under the team plates on the scoreboard. The “Turtle slain”, “Lord slain” and “Turret destroyed” announcements add one automatically. They reset with each new game.
  • Battlefield effect: pick this game’s effect from the dropdown on the Draft or Live game tab (it starts on None), or type one that is not listed. It shows as an icon panel above the draft and in a top corner of the scoreboard overlay (corner and height are in Settings). The list is in js/events.js.
  • Loading screen: loading.html is a full-screen scene for the wait between the draft and the game. The ten picked heroes stay on screen, blue along the top and red along the bottom, and each card takes turns showing the hero, the player and the hero’s season win rate. Between the two rows it rotates through the matchup, how the two drafts compare, the bans with their ban rate, both teams’ results and head to head, and the season’s most contested heroes, skipping any slide that has nothing to show. Seconds per slide is in Settings → Look.
  • Hotkeys: on the Draft tab, Space starts or pauses the timer and → / ← move a phase. On the Live game tab every announcement has a key printed on its button (top letter row for blue, middle row for red, bottom row for the map) and Esc hides the banner. They never fire while you type in a box, and the Hotkeys switch at the top turns them off.
  • Waiting screen: shows both teams, their players, the series score and a countdown. Set the headline and the minutes on the Setup tab and press Start. Like a broadcast pre-show it keeps moving: the matchup takes turns with today’s matches and the standings, the countdown stays in view on every page, and results and coming matches run along the bottom. Both can be switched off in the same card.
  • Centre stage: with the picks on the left and right edges, the middle of the draft overlay can show the Draft story (matchup, whose turn it is, a spotlight on every pick and ban with that hero’s season record, then both lineups), the Season info pages, or Media (videos and images you put in Assets/Media). Choose it in Settings, or switch it off to leave room for player cameras. It follows the show design. Settings also has an Animated background switch that fills the whole draft scene with the design’s moving backdrop; leave it off if a camera or the game should show through.
  • Season: the Season tab holds the teams, their players, the schedule, the bracket and the standings, each on its own sub-tab. Teams and players are chosen from dropdowns everywhere else, so they are typed once. The standings are worked out from the schedule’s scores (untick the box on that sub-tab to type them yourself), and when a series ends its result is written into the schedule and bracket for you. Player photos are matched by nickname; anyone without one shows a template portrait (replace Assets/Other/player-template.svg to change it). Hero records (picked, won, banned) are counted each time you enter who won a game. Save / load exports all of it to a file and imports one back; season-finals.json in this folder is a sample season to try.
  • MVP: on the Live game tab, click the MVP’s hero, type the kills, deaths and assists (and gold, damage and rating unless “KDA only” is ticked), then switch MVP on in the On screen strip at the top. Choose the MVP before pressing who won (they are steps 1 and 2 on that tab). With “Alternate with the winning team” ticked, the screen loops: the winning team for ten seconds, then the MVP for ten seconds.
  • Swap sides moves each team (name, logo, score, nicknames) to the other side.

Good to know

  • The overlays are transparent in OBS. Opened in a normal browser they show a dark backdrop so you can preview them; add ?bg=green to the URL for a chroma-key green background instead.
  • There are seven show designs (Classic, Slant, Glass, Studio, Neon, Prestige and Championship, the finals look with the most animation). One choice, Show design at the top of the Settings tab, restyles the draft, the scoreboard, the MVP screen, the waiting screen and the loading screen together. The draft’s picks can sit along the bottom or down the left and right edges in any design (Picks position in Settings). To preview one, or give a single OBS scene its own look, add it to the URL, for example mvp.html?design=neon.
  • The match is saved in data/state.json, so closing and reopening the server keeps your teams and draft. Reloading a page never loses anything.
  • Updates: each time the server starts it checks GitHub for a newer version and asks before installing one (yes, not now, or skip this version). Only the files that changed are downloaded. Your match and season in the data folder are never touched, and a file you edited yourself is copied to data/backup before it is replaced. With no internet it says so and starts as usual. Update before a show, not during one.
  • A checklist for testing every function before a show is in docs/QA-Test-Cases.pdf.
  • Windows may ask whether to run the server file because it was downloaded. That is expected; untick “Always ask before opening this file” and press Run. To give the window your own name, run tools\build-launcher.ps1 -Publisher "Your Name".
  • New hero: put a 240 × 390 portrait in Assets/HeroPick/ and add one line to js/heroes.js. A hero without a portrait still works and shows its name.
  • The previous version of this tool is kept in the legacy folder.
