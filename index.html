Start

  1. Double-click Start Overlay Server.bat and leave its window open while you stream. The control panel opens in your browser.
  2. In OBS add two Browser sources, both 1920 × 1080:
    SourceURLUse it in
    Drafthttp://localhost:8777/draft.htmlYour draft / pick-ban scene
    Scoreboardhttp://localhost:8777/scoreboard.htmlYour in-game scene
  3. Run everything from the control panel. The overlays update on their own.

Tip: in OBS, Docks → Custom Browser Docks lets you add http://localhost:8777/control.html as a dock so the control panel sits inside OBS.

Running a draft

  • Click a hero (or type part of the name and press Enter) to lock it into the highlighted slot. The draft moves to the next phase by itself and follows the tournament order.
  • Click any slot to fix a mistake, right-click to clear it, and drag one pick onto another to swap them during the adjustment phase.
  • Undo steps back one action. Next game clears the draft and bumps the game number; use the “Games won” buttons to update the series score.
  • Draft mode: Normal or Fearless. In Fearless, heroes picked in earlier games of the series are locked. “Next game” records the finished game’s picks; “Reset series” clears them.
  • Swap sides moves each team (name, logo, score, nicknames) to the other side.

Good to know

  • The overlays are transparent in OBS. Opened in a normal browser they show a dark backdrop so you can preview them; add ?bg=green to the URL for a chroma-key green background instead.
  • Each overlay has six designs, picked in the control panel (Overlay design for the draft, Design for the scoreboard). To give one OBS scene its own look, or just to preview one, add it to the URL, for example draft.html?design=towers.
  • The match is saved in data/state.json, so closing and reopening the server keeps your teams and draft.
  • New hero: put a 240 × 390 portrait in Assets/HeroPick/ and add one line to js/heroes.js. A hero without a portrait still works and shows its name.
  • The previous version of this tool is kept in the legacy folder.
